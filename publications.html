CVPR 2026 · Co-author

Quantum-Gated Task-interaction Knowledge Distillation for Pre-trained Model-based Class-Incremental Learning

Continual Learning Quantum Computing Knowledge Distillation

Key Innovations

  • Parameterize quantum circuits to embed samples and task representations into a high-dimensional Hilbert space, enabling explicit modeling of task interactions beyond Euclidean geometry.
  • Measure sample–task geometric correlation through quantum state fidelity, capturing relational dependencies among task embeddings that dynamically guide both training and inference across streaming tasks.
  • A low-parameter quantum gating mechanism delivers high expressive power, making the method well-suited for memory-constrained continual learning settings.

Achieves state-of-the-art on ImageNet-A with a 2.92% accuracy gain over the strongest prior baselines.

ICASSP 2026 · Co-author

LDE Prompt: Layer-importance guided Dual Expandable Prompt Pool for Pre-trained Model-based Class-Incremental Learning

Continual Learning Prompt Tuning Vision-Language

Key Innovations

  • Quantify each transformer layer's task-representation capability via mutual information gain, adaptively selecting key layers for prompt injection instead of relying on hand-tuned heuristics.
  • Dual expandable prompt pool architecture: a Global Pool preserves historical knowledge while a Training Pool absorbs new task signals, enabling dynamic capacity growth without overwriting prior tasks.
  • Effectively balances plasticity and stability, reducing catastrophic forgetting across long task sequences while maintaining strong forward transfer.

Sets new state-of-the-art on CIFAR-100, CUB-200, and VTAB with 0.50%–3.23% average accuracy improvements.

A Motor-Imagery-based Rehabilitation Assessment Method, System, and Storage Medium — CN120954692A

EEG Cross-modal MoE Agent

Key Innovations

  • End-to-end motor-imagery rehabilitation assessment pipeline that closes the long-standing gap between raw EEG signals and clinical semantics.
  • Optimal-transport-based EEG–text cross-modal alignment, learning a joint geometry between neural signals and clinical descriptions for fine-grained correspondence.
  • MoE decoder architecture distilled from a large foundation model, balancing capacity and inference cost for medical deployment.

Delivers a deployable, agent-driven system that turns raw EEG signals into structured, personalized clinical-grade rehabilitation reports.
