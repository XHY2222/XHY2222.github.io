About

Master's student at the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). My research focuses on LLM Post-Training, Class-incremental Learning, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). I am also passionate about multimodal large models, agent systems, and voice interaction.

I was directly admitted to BUPT for graduate studies. During my undergraduate years, I won multiple awards including First Prize in the National Mathematical Modeling Contest (Beijing Division) and Honorable Mention in the Mathematical Contest in Modeling (MCM). I also completed two national-level innovation projects, demonstrating solid engineering and research capabilities.
