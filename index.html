Publications

CVPR 2026 · Second Author
Quantum-Gated Task-interaction Knowledge Distillation for Pre-trained Model-based Class-Incremental Learning

We propose a Quantum-Gated Task-interaction Knowledge Distillation framework that parameterizes quantum circuits to embed samples and task representations into high-dimensional Hilbert space. This enables explicit measurement of sample-task geometric correlation through quantum state fidelity. Our method achieves SOTA performance on ImageNet-A (2.92% improvement over baselines), demonstrating the expressive power of quantum mechanisms under low-parameter increments.

Continual Learning Quantum Computing Knowledge Distillation
ICASSP 2026 · Second Author
LDE Prompt: Layer-importance guided Dual Expandable Prompt Pool for Pre-trained Model-based Class-Incremental Learning

We propose a layer-importance guided dual expandable prompt pool framework that quantifies task representation capabilities via mutual information gain, adaptively selecting key layers for prompt injection. Our dual-pool architecture (Global Pool + Training Pool) enables dynamic prompt expansion and historical knowledge protection. Achieves SOTA on CIFAR-100, CUB-200, and VTAB with average accuracy improvements of 0.50%–3.23%.

Addressing the challenge of insufficient mapping between EEG signals and clinical semantics, I led the development of an end-to-end motor-imagery rehabilitation assessment system.
