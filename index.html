Xiao Huiyu

I am a Master's student in Information and Communication Engineering at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), starting in September 2026, advised by Prof. Zhenyu Wu. I received my Bachelor's degree in Electronic Information Engineering from BUPT. I also attended the Summer School on Data Science at the University of Cambridge, UK.

My recent research focuses on Continual Learning, especially Class-Incremental Learning. I recently proposed QKD (Quantum-Gated Task-interaction Knowledge Distillation), a framework that leverages quantum gating to model relational dependencies among task embeddings, dynamically capturing sample-to-task relevance for both training and inference across streaming tasks. I believe models will soon be able to evolve autonomously, continuously learning new knowledge, changing the world, and making our lives better. For more about my details, please feel free to check out my Publications, Photography, Cooking, Gaming, and Awards.
